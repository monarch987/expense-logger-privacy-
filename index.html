Privacy Policy

App: expense-logger-app  ·  Effective: 18 October 2025  ·  Last updated: 18 October 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how expense-logger-app (the “App”), published by Akshay Bhujang (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the App.

The App is a personal cashbook and expense logger. Your accounts, transactions, and notes are stored on your device. We do not operate sign-in or cloud sync for your cashbook in the current version. This version of the App does not show advertisements.

Privacy questions and grievances: expense.logger.987@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

You create and manage the following on your device:

Data Examples Where it is stored
Cashbook accounts Account name, opening balance, running balance On-device database
Transactions Amount, income/expense type, category, date/time, optional notes On-device database
Categories Predefined and app-managed category records On-device database
App preferences Dark mode, preferred currency On-device settings storage

Notes you add to a transaction stay on your device unless you export or share them (for example as a PDF).

The App uses a local placeholder record for database structure only (not a real email login). We do not collect your email address to create an account.

2.2 Information collected automatically

We do not run our own analytics, crash reporting, or advertising in this version of the App. We do not send your cashbook data to our servers (there is no app-owned backend for your logs).

2.3 Information from permissions

Permission / capability Purpose
Notifications (Android, when saving a PDF if not already allowed) Show a local “PDF saved” message with the save location. Notifications are local only; we do not send remote push messages.
File storage & sharing (export PDF) Save a PDF in the App’s storage and open the system share sheet so you can save or send the file to an app you choose.

The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library access.

2.4 Information from third parties

There is no Google, Apple, phone, or email sign-in. We do not receive profile data from third-party login providers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Purpose Data used
Run the cashbook (accounts, transactions, search, analytics) Local accounts, transactions, categories, notes
Remember your preferences Dark mode, currency
Export statements Local transaction data rendered into a PDF on your device
Confirm a PDF was saved Local notification text (path or message)
Answer support or privacy emails you send us Whatever you include in your message

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal data

We do not sell your cashbook data or personal information.

Third parties

  • Apps you choose when sharing a PDF: If you use the share sheet, the PDF (which may include account name, dates, amounts, and notes) is handled by the app or service you select.
  • Our servers: We do not upload your cashbook to an app-owned cloud database.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety—typically limited to correspondence you send us, since we do not hold your cashbook on our servers.

5. Information Visible to Other Users

Nothing in the App is visible to other users. There are no public profiles, feeds, or messaging.

If you share an exported PDF through another app, that content is visible according to that service and the people you send it to.

6. Data Retention

Data Retention
Local cashbook (accounts, transactions, notes) Until you delete them in the App, clear app storage, or uninstall
App settings Until you change them, clear storage, or uninstall
Exported PDFs in app storage Until you delete them or uninstall / clear storage
Emails you send us Only as long as needed to respond, then deleted or anonymized when no longer required

Uninstalling the App removes on-device data in the usual way for your operating system (subject to backups you enable).

7. Account and Data Deletion

The App does not create cloud user accounts. All cashbook data lives on your device. You delete data inside the App—there is no separate web account to close.

Delete data in the App

  1. Transactions: Open a log and use the delete action to remove that log and its items.
  2. Accounts: Open Accounts, select an account, and delete it. Deleting an account also removes its transactions from the local database.

Delete everything on the device

To remove all App data at once: clear the App’s storage in system settings, or uninstall expense-logger-app. This removes the local database, settings, and PDFs saved in the App’s files.

We cannot remotely wipe your phone. If you emailed us personal information, contact expense.logger.987@gmail.com to ask us to delete that correspondence.

If Google Play asks for a data-deletion URL, you may use this page’s address with this section: #account-and-data-deletion.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal data.

In the App you can:

  • Edit or delete transactions and accounts
  • Change currency and dark mode in Settings
  • Turn off notification permission in system settings (PDF feedback may use an on-screen alert instead)
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data

For information you sent us by email, contact expense.logger.987@gmail.com.

9. Data Security

  • Cashbook data is stored in the App’s on-device database and preference storage.
  • We do not transmit cashbook contents to an app-owned server in this version.

No storage method is perfectly secure. Anyone with access to your unlocked device, or copies in device backups, could see local data. Use your device lock screen and backup settings you are comfortable with.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information, email us and we will delete it.

11. International Data Transfers

Your cashbook stays on your device (and in any cloud backup you configure at the OS level). We do not host your cashbook on our servers in this version.

This policy does not cover other apps or websites you use when you share a PDF or leave the App.
