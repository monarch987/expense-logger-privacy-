In short

  • Your accounts, transactions, and notes are stored on your device. We never receive them.
  • There is no sign-in and no cloud sync.
  • The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes and to understand how the App performs, so future updates are easier to use.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Ledger (the “App”), published by Monarch987 (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the App.

The App is a personal cashbook and expense logger. Your accounts, transactions, and notes are stored on your device. We do not operate sign-in or cloud sync for your cashbook in the current version.

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to monitor stability and Google AdMob to show advertisements. Both are described in sections 2.2, 4, and 13.

Privacy questions and grievances: expense.logger.987@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

You create and manage the following on your device:

DataExamplesWhere it is stored
Cashbook accounts Account name, opening balance, running balance On-device database
Transactions Amount, income/expense type, category, date/time, optional notes On-device database
Categories Predefined and app-managed category records On-device database
App preferences Dark mode, preferred currency On-device settings storage

Notes you add to a transaction stay on your device unless you export or share them (for example as a PDF).

The App uses a local placeholder record for database structure only (not a real email login). We do not collect your email address to create an account.

2.2 Information collected automatically

We do not send your cashbook data to our servers. There is no app-owned backend for your logs. The App does include two Google services that collect limited technical information automatically:

ServiceWhat it may collectWhy
Firebase Crashlytics (Google) Crash reports and error logs, device model, operating system version, App version, device state at the time of a crash, and a Firebase installation identifier To find and fix crashes and to understand how the App is handled in everyday use, so we can make the experience easier in future updates. It is not used to collect your financial data or to profile you.
Google AdMob (Google) Advertising ID, IP address, device and OS information, and ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks To show and measure ads. Ad revenue helps us keep the App available.

We do not intentionally include your accounts, transactions, or notes in crash reports. Google processes this information under its own policies, linked in section 13.

2.3 Information from permissions

Permission / capabilityPurpose
Notifications (Android, when saving a PDF if not already allowed) Show a local “PDF saved” message with the save location. Notifications are local only; we do not send remote push messages.
File storage & sharing (export PDF) Save a PDF in the App’s storage and open the system share sheet so you can save or send the file to an app you choose.
Internet access and advertising ID Needed to deliver ads and to send crash reports. The cashbook itself works offline.

The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library access. Google’s ad services may infer an approximate region from your IP address.

2.4 Information from third parties

There is no Google, Apple, phone, or email sign-in. We do not receive profile data from third-party login providers.

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal data

We do not sell your cashbook data or personal information.

Third parties

  • Google (Firebase Crashlytics): receives crash reports and technical device information so we can diagnose problems.
  • Google (AdMob): receives device and advertising identifiers and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your device settings and, where required, your consent choice.
  • Apps you choose when sharing a PDF: if you use the share sheet, the PDF (which may include account name, dates, amounts, and notes) is handled by the app or service you select.
  • Our servers: we do not upload your cashbook to an app-owned cloud database.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety. This is typically limited to correspondence you send us, since we do not hold your cashbook on our servers.

5. Information Visible to Other Users

Nothing in the App is visible to other users. There are no public profiles, feeds, or messaging.

If you share an exported PDF through another app, that content is visible according to that service and the people you send it to.

6. Data Retention

7. Account and Data Deletion

The App does not create cloud user accounts. All cashbook data lives on your device. You delete data inside the App. There is no separate web account to close.

Delete data in the App

  1. Transactions: Open a log and use the delete action to remove that log and its items.
  2. Accounts: Open Accounts, select an account, and delete it. Deleting an account also removes its transactions from the local database.

Delete everything on the device

To remove all App data at once, clear the App’s storage in system settings or uninstall Ledger. This removes the local database, settings, and PDFs saved in the App’s files.

Crash and advertising data

Crash reports and advertising data are held by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Google settings. To ask us to delete correspondence you sent, or to raise a request about crash data linked to your installation, email expense.logger.987@gmail.com.

We cannot remotely wipe your phone.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal data.

In the App and on your device you can:

  • Edit or delete transactions and accounts
  • Change currency and dark mode in Settings
  • Turn off notification permission in system settings (PDF feedback may use an on-screen alert instead)
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device’s Google settings, or at Google Ads Settings
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data

For information you sent us by email, contact expense.logger.987@gmail.com.

9. Data Security

  • Cashbook data is stored in the App’s on-device database and preference storage.
  • We do not transmit cashbook contents to an app-owned server.
  • Crash reports and ad requests are sent to Google over encrypted connections.

No storage method is perfectly secure. Anyone with access to your unlocked device, or copies in device backups, could see local data. Use your device lock screen and backup settings you are comfortable with.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and the App’s ads are not intended for them. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information, email us and we will delete it.

11. International Data Transfers

Your cashbook stays on your device (and in any cloud backup you configure at the OS level). We do not host it on our servers.

Google may process crash and advertising data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under its own safeguards and policies.
